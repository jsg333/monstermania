## One thing worth knowing

Every real problem in this game so far was found by you and Dad playing it — not by my tests. The tests are good at checking the game does what I told it to do. They're useless at noticing that a level is boring, or that a coin is impossible to reach, or that clicking a button does the wrong thing.

That's your job, and you're good at it.
